Our story

A small kitchen with a big oven

Ember & Oak started with a single oak-fired oven and a simple rule: cook what's in season, buy from people we know, and never rush the fire. Our menu changes with the weeks, and so does our specials board.

  • 01

    Local first

    Produce, dairy and meat from nearby farms wherever we can.

  • 02

    Made in-house

    Pasta, bread, sauces and desserts are made fresh every day.

  • 03

    Open fire

    Oak and olive wood, never gas, for flavor you can't fake.
